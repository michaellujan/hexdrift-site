HEXDRIFT

A one-thumb hex-merge puzzle for iPhone.

Privacy policySupport

How it plays

Drag a floating hex tile onto the board. Same-color, same-level neighbors merge into it and level up, and a merge that lands beside another match keeps chaining. Then the board turns one notch. Daily gives everyone the same 90-second board; Endless runs until the board fills.

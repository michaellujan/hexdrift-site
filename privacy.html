Privacy policy

Hexdrift · last updated September 18, 2026

Hexdrift does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in.
  • The app makes no network requests of its own. In-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit, and Apple's privacy policy covers those transactions.
  • Scores, settings, and purchase records are stored only on your device.
  • If Game Center is enabled in a future version, leaderboard scores are handled by Apple's Game Center under Apple's terms.
  • The app contains no advertising and no analytics.

Questions: see the support page.
